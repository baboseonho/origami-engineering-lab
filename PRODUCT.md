# Product

**Origami Engineering Lab** — an interactive computational-origami textbook and simulator for KAIST AE53006 *Origami Engineering* (Lectures 1–5).

## Audience and scene
KAIST engineering students studying the course, mostly the week before the exam: laptop at a desk or in the library, daytime and evening, sessions of 30–90 minutes, switching between the lecture slides and the app.

## Job
Make the lecture's abstract geometry physically obvious by direct manipulation: drive one crease and watch the coupled fold angles, see when a request is not rigid-feasible and why, compare rigid kinematics with energy-based deformation, and check theorems (Kawasaki, Maekawa, N − 3M, generalized flat-foldability) on live models.

## Truths that must survive any redesign
- Fold angles are coupled: one driving crease, everything else solved; infeasibility is reported, never faked.
- Flat-foldable, rigid-foldable, currently rigid-feasible, collision-free and elastic-feasible stay separate labels.
- Sign convention: ρ = 0 flat, ρ > 0 valley, ρ < 0 mountain. Mountain = red, Valley = blue (domain convention, also in the legend).
- Prescribed paths are labelled as such, never as solver output.

## Language
Korean UI and explanations; lecture terminology and symbols in English (Kawasaki, Maekawa, ρ, θ, DoF, rank J) so they match the slides and the exam.

## Brand commitments
Mode: Operate (it is a working tool). Visual world chosen by the user: **drafting table** (engineering drawing set). Calmer, focus-first density.
