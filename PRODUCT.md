# Product

**Origami BaBolaB** — an interactive *Origami Engineering* textbook and simulator for everyone, in Korean and English.

## Audience and scene
Anyone curious about how folding works: hobbyists who want to fold real models, students meeting the geometry for the first time, and engineers or designers looking for the theory and tools. Laptop or phone, sessions of 10–90 minutes, often with a sheet of paper at hand.

## Job
Make the abstract geometry of folding physically obvious by direct manipulation, starting from a structure the reader cares about (a shopping bag, a map, a stent, a crash box): drive one crease and watch the coupled fold angles, see when a request is not rigid-feasible and why, compare rigid kinematics with energy-based deformation, and check theorems (Kawasaki, Maekawa, N − 3M, generalized flat-foldability) on live models.

## Truths that must survive any redesign
- Fold angles are coupled: one driving crease, everything else solved; infeasibility is reported, never faked.
- Flat-foldable, rigid-foldable, currently rigid-feasible, collision-free and elastic-feasible stay separate labels.
- Sign convention: ρ = 0 flat, ρ > 0 valley, ρ < 0 mountain. Mountain = red, Valley = blue (domain convention, also in the legend).
- Prescribed paths are labelled as such, never as solver output.

## Language
Korean and English, switchable at any time; theorem names and symbols keep their usual form in both (Kawasaki, Maekawa, ρ, θ, DoF, rank J). Avoid course codes and institution names in the product: the field is called "Origami Engineering".

## Brand commitments
Mode: Operate (it is a working tool). Visual world chosen by the user: **drafting table** (engineering drawing set). Calmer, focus-first density.
